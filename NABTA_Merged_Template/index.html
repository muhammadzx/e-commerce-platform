Meet the plant assistant ↗
What can I find at Nabta?

Browse plants, pots, soil and nutrition, garden tools, botanical decor, and plant-care products. Each product page includes its own details.

How do I choose a plant for my space?

Start with the light available and the room you want to green up. The plant assistant interface lets you describe your space and budget.

Can I sell my own plants or supplies?

Yes. Open Sell with Nabta to prepare a listing with product details, photos, and a price. Listings are reviewed before publication in the planned marketplace flow.

What should I check before buying a treatment?

Read the verified product label, intended use, and safety information. Treatment listings in this design are illustrative; product-specific instructions will be supplied by the seller.

Where can I follow my order?

Your account includes an orders page with order details and status.
